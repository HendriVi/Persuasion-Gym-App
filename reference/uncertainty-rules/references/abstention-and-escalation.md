# Abstention, Review, and Escalation

## Abstain when evidence cannot support the requested resolution

Preferred outputs include:

- `UNKNOWN`
- `UNRESOLVED`
- `NOT_ASSESSED`
- `DEPENDENCY_INCOMPLETE`
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `REANALYSIS_REQUIRED`

These are successful outcomes when the evidence warrants them.

## Escalation triggers

Trigger specialist review when:

1. two canonical labels remain materially plausible and the distinction changes training feedback;
2. the factual status of a load-bearing claim changes;
3. source provenance or label-to-value mapping is disputed;
4. person-specific causal language would exceed available evidence;
5. intent is consequential but only weakly inferred;
6. a system loop depends on a speculative edge;
7. a training answer key would require scoring a disputed dimension;
8. a new source materially changes evidence strength or scope.

## Review discipline

The reviewing specialist must issue a new versioned assertion/object. Do not mutate historical records silently.
