# Scope, Transportability, and Generalization

## Scope fields

Where material, assertions should specify:

- population and sampling frame;
- cultural/language context;
- task or decision;
- setting;
- medium/platform;
- time window;
- exposure/repetition structure;
- measurement definition;
- outcome horizon.

## Transport rule

When an assertion is moved beyond the source evidence scope, record:

- source scope;
- destination scope;
- which dimensions changed;
- transport rationale;
- likely effect modifiers;
- why transport could fail;
- `EXTRAPOLATED` among the evidence bases.

Do not phrase transported evidence as if it were directly demonstrated in the destination population/context.

## Ecological and individual inference

- Group-level association does not establish an individual-level mechanism.
- Between-person association does not automatically establish within-person dynamics.
- Individual-level mechanism evidence does not automatically establish population-scale consequences.
- Platform-specific findings do not automatically transfer to other platforms or ranking systems.
