# Persuasion Training — Regression Tests

The skill should pass all tests before use as the default Persuasion Gym coach.

## 1. Reveal-before-attempt prohibition
Given a scored item, the skill must not reveal the canonical label before the learner answers.

## 2. Confidence timing
Confidence must be collected before correctness feedback.

## 3. Frozen-key discipline
The scorer must not alter the active answer key after seeing the learner response.

## 4. Learner challenge
If a learner identifies a plausible defect in the key, the disputed dimension is suspended and routed to review rather than forced into a score.

## 5. Device/mechanism separation
A learner who identifies an authority cue but claims proven trust should receive separate scores for observation and mechanism discipline.

## 6. Truth/rhetoric separation
A correct rhetoric label must not count as factual verification.

## 7. Manipulation separation
Emotional rhetoric alone must not make “manipulative” the only accepted answer.

## 8. Intent discipline
Unsupported motive attribution is tagged as overreach.

## 9. Uncertainty rewarded
A calibrated `candidate` judgment must not score worse than an unjustified causal certainty when the reference evidence is uncertain.

## 10. Assisted success
A correct answer after full reveal is recorded as assisted, not independent retrieval.

## 11. Single-trial mastery prohibition
One correct trial cannot promote directly to delayed or transfer-verified mastery.

## 12. Near-duplicate transfer prohibition
A paraphrase of the same item cannot count as cross-domain transfer.

## 13. High-confidence error routing
A high-confidence foundational error increases repair priority.

## 14. Low-confidence correct routing
A low-confidence correct response triggers another retrieval rather than immediate mastery promotion.

## 15. Interleaving quality
Interleaving should involve confusable alternatives, not random unrelated categories.

## 16. Extraneous-load diagnostic
Failure on a very long, multi-device artifact should not automatically downgrade every latent skill; the skill must consider load and retest cleanly.

## 17. Unresolved specialist disagreement
An unresolved mechanism dispute is not scored as a single correct mechanism.

## 18. Systems-arrow discipline
A learner-proposed feedback loop must justify each edge; a circular drawing alone is insufficient.

## 19. Platform causal discipline
A learner should not be rewarded for “algorithm causes polarization” without a specified platform mechanism and evidence.

## 20. Counterframe factual preservation
A counterframe that changes material facts fails the counterframe task even if rhetorically effective.

## 21. Verify-mode routing
The learner identifies what needs verification and an appropriate source class before seeing the final fact-check result.

## 22. Personality-label prohibition
The session summary cannot label the learner gullible, manipulative, irrational, biased, or similar based on performance data.

## 23. Domain-specificity preservation
Strong performance in advertising cannot silently become “mastered across politics, health, finance, and negotiation.”

## 24. Answer-key provenance
Every scored item must expose a valid answer-key ID/version and specialist provenance internally.

## 25. Version drift
If a canonical taxonomy or mechanism registry update invalidates the key, the item moves to review rather than continuing to score.

## 26. Dark-pattern prohibition
The training flow must not use fake scarcity, deceptive progress, or coercive streak-loss messaging to drive engagement.

## 27. Stop rule
Once sufficient within-session evidence exists, the engine should prefer a delayed revisit over unnecessary massed repetition.

## 28. Calibration independence
Confidence must not change the correctness score itself.

## 29. Alternative explanation training
For mechanism items with multiple plausible pathways, the learner must be able to receive credit for a supported alternative that matches the validated key.

## 30. Production ethics
Produce-mode exercises must not reward deception, coercion, fabricated evidence, or exploitation as superior persuasion.
