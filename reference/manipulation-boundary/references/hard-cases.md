# Hard Cases

## Fear appeal
A fear appeal is not manipulation by default. Assess whether threat information is truthful, proportionate, decision-relevant, and accompanied by meaningful deliberative opportunity. Disproportionate or fabricated threat pressure may support manipulation risk.

## Scarcity
Real and clearly disclosed scarcity is not manipulation. False, fabricated, resetting, or strategically obscured scarcity may support deception and manufactured urgency.

## Social proof
Visible popularity is not manipulation by itself. Fabricated, purchased, non-independent, or misleadingly represented consensus may support deception or credibility theater.

## Authority
Relevant expertise transparently identified is not manipulation. False credentials, irrelevant prestige presented as expertise, or fabricated independence may support credibility theater/deception.

## Defaults
Defaults are not manipulative per se. Concern rises with opacity, difficult reversal, material asymmetry, or exploitation of predictable inertia for hidden interests.

## Repetition
Repetition is not manipulation by itself. Concern rises when repetition is coordinated with source concealment, fabricated independence, information flooding, or suppression of meaningful comparison.

## Emotional narrative
Emotional engagement is not manipulation by default. Assess truthfulness, proportionality, concealment, vulnerability exploitation, and whether arousal materially suppresses meaningful deliberation.

## Negotiation pressure
Deadlines and leverage can be ordinary negotiation. Coercion/manipulation concern rises when penalties, threats, fabricated constraints, dependency, or inability to exit materially undermine voluntariness.

## Political rhetoric
Partisanship, identity, moralization, or strong framing are not manipulation by default. The same evidence standards apply regardless of political alignment.

## Therapeutic / educational influence
Beneficent intent does not immunize influence from manipulation. Transparency, consent, proportionality, autonomy, and vulnerability exploitation remain relevant.
