# Manipulation Boundary — Regression Tests

A compliant implementation must pass at least these tests.

1. Emotional language alone is not classified as manipulation.
2. A false factual claim alone does not automatically yield a manipulation verdict.
3. A verified true claim can still participate in manipulation through other dimensions.
4. Framing alone does not establish manipulation.
5. A nudge/default alone does not establish manipulation.
6. Easy-to-reverse transparent defaults remain low manipulation evidence absent other factors.
7. False scarcity can support deception + manufactured urgency.
8. Unverified scarcity yields indeterminate manufactured-urgency status rather than a guessed verdict.
9. Coercion is explicitly labeled when threats/penalties materially constrain alternatives.
10. Social pressure is not coercion merely because it is uncomfortable.
11. Identity appeal alone does not establish social-sanction pressure.
12. Fabricated social proof can support deception and credibility theater.
13. Accurate, transparently sourced expert consensus is not credibility theater by default.
14. Hidden material fees can support material omission and deception.
15. Asymmetric cancellation friction can support choice-architecture opacity.
16. Personalization alone is not manipulation.
17. Vulnerability exploitation requires evidence of the relevant vulnerability and its exploitation.
18. Group membership alone cannot establish vulnerability exploitation.
19. Beneficent intent does not rule out manipulation.
20. Manipulative intent does not prove person-specific effect.
21. Harm does not automatically establish manipulation.
22. Manipulation does not automatically establish harm.
23. Analytical manipulation status is never automatically converted into a legal conclusion.
24. Missing decisive evidence produces `INDETERMINATE` or dimension `UNKNOWN`.
25. Upstream fact-check contradictions trigger reanalysis instead of silent rewriting.
26. The same rule is applied regardless of political alignment.
27. Rhetorical intensity does not determine severity.
28. Severity refers to impairment of informed/voluntary choice.
29. Multiple moderate dimensions may interact non-additively; no naive score summation is required.
30. `LOW_EVIDENCE_OF_MANIPULATION` is not described as proof of absence.
31. Persuasion, deception, manipulation, coercion, and exploitation remain conceptually separable.
32. A story can be persuasive and emotional without manipulation.
33. Real disclosed time limits do not count as manufactured urgency.
34. Repeated prompts may be manipulation-risk relevant when they materially impede prior choice.
35. Evidence/credibility theater requires mismatch between appearance of support and underlying support.
36. Coercive pressure cannot be inferred solely from a strong call to action.
37. Material omission must be decision-relevant, not merely any omitted detail.
38. Choice architecture opacity requires material distortion/impairment, not merely non-neutral design.
39. Intent status remains independent from overall manipulation risk.
40. Positive dimension findings carry anchored evidence refs in the common schema.
